Shade We Inherited

Somewhere there is a tree that shaped a little piece of your life, whether you sat in its shade, picked its fruit or watched the world go by from under it. Someone planted it, or it came up on its own, and you got to enjoy it. Pin it on the map with a line or three about what it meant to you, and together we'll map the shade we inherited.

1 Tap the map where your tree stands

Schools, campuses, streets, parks, farms and forests all count. Zoom in and switch to Satellite to find the exact tree, and please keep your home off the map.
