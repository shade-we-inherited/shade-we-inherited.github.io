# Shade We Inherited

A shared map of the trees that shaped us. Somewhere there is a tree whose shade you sat in, whose fruit you picked or under which you watched the world go by. Someone planted it, or it came up on its own, and you got to enjoy it. Pin it on the map with a line or three about what it meant to you.

**Add your tree:** https://uceseoo.github.io/shade-we-inherited/

Pins appear after a quick check, usually within a day. Please pin trees in public or shared places and keep homes off the map.

## How it works

- `index.html` is the whole site: a Leaflet map with OpenStreetMap and Esri World Imagery base maps, place search through OpenStreetMap Nominatim, and a short form.
- New pins are sent to a Google Apps Script web app (`Code.gs`), which adds them to a Google Sheet owned by the project. Nothing is public until it has been approved, and the map only receives approved pins.

The stories become Day 16 (Collaborative map) of Elias Omondi's #30DayMapChallenge on 16 November 2026.
